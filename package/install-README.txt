Put Max Payne 3's files here (this is PPSA95454/install/), then open the app:
it installs them by itself, one at a time, and shows the progress on its HOME
page.

- The game: your two disc images (.iso, any names), or a folder of each
  disc's files (default.xex and the rest). The app tells disc 1 and disc 2
  apart by itself.
- Title Update 9 (required): its package file, tu00000001_00000000 (any
  name). It patches both discs. Other title updates can't be used.
- DLC (optional): the DLC package files (any names), as many as you have.
- HD textures (optional): the Nexus texture pack, see below.
- Saves (optional): Xbox 360 save packages; import them from the SAVES page.

Archives (.7z, .rar, .zip) aren't opened on the PS5: extract them on a PC
first (7-Zip), and copy what's inside here. When everything is installed, the
app asks whether to delete the disc images, title update and DLC packages it
installed from (X) or keep them here (O: they aren't installed again). The
texture pack's files are always deleted once the HD textures are made.

HD textures (optional)
----------------------
The PC version's high resolution textures, from the Nexus texture pack
(nexusmods.com/maxpayne3/mods/275), made into the app's own on the PS5.

1. On the mod's FILES page, download every file: the main file (Nexus
   Version) and all the optional ones (Chapter 3 Minor Fixes, Chapter 9
   Minor Repairs, Chapter 10 Minor Fixes, Overall stability improved).
2. Extract each one on a PC with 7-Zip, each into its own folder (7-Zip's
   "Extract to <name>\"). There's no need to merge them or overwrite
   anything: the app finds the right copy of every file by itself. (Already
   merged them into one folder, as the mod's own instructions say? That
   works too.)
3. Copy those folders (or your merged one) here, into PPSA95454/install/.
4. Open the app: it makes the HD textures (about 8 GB, in PPSA95454/textures/)
   and deletes the pack's files afterwards. It needs about 9 GB free while it
   works, on top of the pack itself.

That gives 28,971 of the 29,782 HD textures. The other 811 (3%: surfaces
shared between levels and some interiors and props, which the pack doesn't
replace) come from the PC game's own files; without them those few show the
Xbox 360's texture. To have them too, copy your PC game's folder (the one
with MaxPayne3.exe) here as well. Turn the HD textures on or off on the
SETTINGS page.

The app's CONTENT page shows what's installed and what's waiting here, and
installs (X) or uninstalls (triangle) each part. Backups made on the SAVES
page go to install/backups/.
